# Energy Core — mining simulator prototype

A parts-based Roblox prototype with electric mining, ore collection, training, shops, and an NPC ore buyer.

## Play

With Rojo connected, stop and press **Play** to run the latest scripts. The Scrap Zapper equips automatically. The colorful parts-built lobby and mine shell are generated by the server; each player's blocks are generated locally when they join.

- Walk from the cyan spawn pad through the lobby's MINE arch to the square mine. The orange weapon stand, purple upgrade stand, and NPC sell stand sit along the path.
- Hold left click over a block to channel flickering electricity. When it breaks, the zapper blasts that block plus its two nearest neighbors and throws debris and material chunks from all three.
- Move within 18 studs of a material chunk to pull it magnetically into the player. The material HUD tracks each ore separately.
- Successful pickups create simulator-style popups that combine rapid collections of the same material before fading.
- Walk into excavated spaces and aim downward to descend. Five strata, 60 rows and 360 studs deep. Buried blocks render when uncovered.
- Press **R** or **Surface** to return to the deck.
- Click **Reset Mine** to refill your own grid and return to the surface. Reset has a five-second cooldown.
- Click with the zapper equipped outside the mine to gain Power and XP. Training responds immediately and supports 25 clicks per second, with server validation and a short cooldown after combat. Clicking blocks or the stabilizer never trains. Breaking blocks also grants XP. Power increases mining damage, and Level 2 unlocks the Arc Pistol.
- Talk to the **Ore Buyer** NPC on the surface to sell your whole bag. The purple **UPGRADES** storefront increases bag capacity by 25 (first price $50). The orange **WEAPON SHOP** sells the Arc Pistol for $150 and equips it again for free after purchase.
- On touch devices, aim using the center crosshair and hold **Hold to Zap**. Gamepad uses center aim and the tool activation trigger. These input paths still need device playtesting.

## Scope

12 × 12 × 18 locally rendered blocks per player, server-owned per-player health, two part-built tools, server-validated range/accessibility/damage/collection, a three-block mining blast, five material types, magnetic pickups, NPC selling, cash, storage and weapon shops, material inventory, Power/Level/XP progression, target health, block counter, and depth HUD. Mine reset affects only the requesting player. Sound assets are still pending. The branching lightning is visual only.

## Tune

Edit `src/shared/Config.luau`: block count/size, mining range, damage tick, damage, layer colors and health. Keep Depth divisible by the number of layers. Default damage is 9 every 0.12 seconds, and health is 36 / 45 / 54.

Build after source changes:

```sh
rojo build -o ProjectMilka.rbxlx
```

For live source sync, run `rojo serve` and connect the Studio Rojo plugin.

The lobby is editable under `Workspace.Lobby` in Studio. The fixed mine deck, rim, shaft walls, bedrock, and entrance gate are editable under `Workspace.MineArea`. They were created with `studio/build_lobby.lua` and `studio/build_mine_area.lua`, which can rebuild them in a fresh place. The source-controlled services provide a fallback if an authored lobby or mine area is missing. The 2,592 ore blocks still render separately for each player during Play; they must not be saved as shared Workspace parts. Keep the mine opening aligned to the grid at world origin when editing the shell. To swap a shop stand, replace its Model in `Workspace.Lobby` while keeping the name `UpgradeStore` or `WeaponStore` and a `Counter` BasePart; the server reuses its ProximityPrompt. The existing `showcase npc` sits at the sell stand.

For editable UI matching the visual direction, paste `studio/build_surface_hud.lua`, `studio/build_progress_hud.lua`, and `studio/build_shop_gui.lua` into Studio's Command Bar in Edit mode, then paste `studio/expand_shop_gui.lua` for the scrolling upgrade and weapon catalogs. They create editable GUIs under `StarterGui`; Rojo does not own those GUIs. Run `studio/setup_sell_npc.lua` once to position and prepare your existing `Workspace["showcase npc"]`. Selling uses your `ReplicatedStorage.DialogModule` and `StarterGui.dialog`. Shop prompts open the GUI; purchases require the GUI buttons.

## Playtest checklist

1. Press Play and verify the tool equips and Output has no errors.
2. Hold on one block: lightning appears, health drops, one block breaks and the counter rises once.
3. Hold while sweeping across blocks; release and confirm damage/lighting stops.
4. Mine down through gray, teal and purple strata; check walking/falling and target visibility in a narrow shaft.
5. Surface from the bottom; reset and confirm all blocks return.
6. Unequip, respawn, and re-equip; ensure input still works.
7. In a two-player Studio session, mine a block as player A. Verify the same block still exists for player B. Reset A's mine and verify B's mine stays excavated.
8. Click the tool away from the mine; confirm Power and XP increase. Break blocks and confirm XP rises. Reach Level 2.
9. Mine until the bag is full, surface, talk to the Ore Buyer NPC, choose to sell, and confirm ore counts clear and cash rises by the listed material values.
10. Buy the first bag upgrade at the purple storefront; capacity should rise from 50 to 75 and cash should fall by $50. Confirm the next price becomes $100.
11. Save $150, use the orange weapon storefront, and verify the Arc Pistol equips. Click again and confirm Power rises by 3 per click.
12. Try touch emulation and verify targeting, pickups, NPC dialogue, and store prompts.

Validation performed: Rojo place build, Studio launch, click-to-train HUD check, and an Ore Buyer dialogue/empty-bag sale in Play mode. Selling a nonempty bag and purchasing upgrades still need an in-game playtest.

## Unknown Core discovery

Breaking blocks can uncover a personal Unknown Core. The first is guaranteed by the twelfth broken block before a player has ever collected one. Provisional chances are 1.5%, 2.5%, and 4% per broken block across the three current layers; grades are Cracked or Charged. Tune these values in `Config.Cores`.

Hold E (or use the touch/controller prompt) near the glowing discovery to pick it up. Core storage has three slots independent of ore capacity. The server validates ownership, distance, live mine blocks, static obstructions, capacity, and one-time collection. Full storage leaves the discovery claimable for three minutes, with explicit warnings. Mine reset clears unclaimed discoveries, but stored cores survive reset, death, and ore sales. Collected records are included in the existing saved profile; Studio saving remains disabled by `Config.Persistence.EnableInStudio`.

`CoreService` owns discovery and collection; `CoreController` draws private placeholder drops and binds the prompt. Collected cores appear as individual Tools in the Roblox hotbar and can be held. Tools are restored from saved core records when joining or respawning; syncing preserves equipped Tools and prevents duplicates. `studio/build_core_hud.lua` disables the previous HUD. One-slot stabilization and eight revealed core rewards are implemented; see `docs/stabilizer-integration.md` for the flow and model contract.

Studio checks: guaranteed first discovery, distant pickup rejection, nearby pickup, duplicate rejection, three-slot capacity, core storage totals, and preservation across ore sale/mine reset. Production DataStore round-trip and multiplayer visibility still need playtesting.
